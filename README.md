# CorePrint
A Klipper CoreXY printer - for a good 3D printing experience at the start, then pushing the speeds to their limits later on.

## Why?
My old i3 Mega Printer breaks everytime I fix it. I can't afford to keep repairing those old parts so I thought of a CoreXY printer with a Print Head Swapper which is quick.

## Inspiration
I took the frame of the https://github.com/SnakeOilXY/SnakeOil-XY because it is similar to most other 3D printer frames and is pretty good in my opinion. The only difference between this frame from other frames is how the Z counterweight is built.
I used the toolhead from https://github.com/Armchair-Heavy-Industries/Xol-Toolhead
XOL-Toolhead because it is known and used a lot already and its only 7 easy screws, making the toolheads both stable and easy to replace. I'm trying too make a easy to use printer compatible with many stuff, not a propietary printer with no guides or anything.

## What about Endstops?
I am using sensorless homing for the X and Y axis. They are reliable enough and for Z I will be using a Klicky PCB Z Probe.
## Usage of AI
I used AI for research, part comparisons, picking of some of the parts and verifying that I didn't do any mistakes.
